Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need to know coding or AI already?

No. The cohort starts from Python fundamentals and progressively introduces more advanced AI engineering concepts.

How much live teaching is there?

16 live classes of 2 hours each, two a week over 8 weeks, which is 32+ hours of instructor-led teaching, plus weekly assignments and project work.

What will I have built by the end?

Three guided projects (an AI chatbot, a full-stack AI application and a RAG knowledge assistant) and an individual capstone application that you deploy and present.

What is included for free?

Free access to 30+ courses on the Trafy app and the Trafy Employability Index test, along with a Trafy profile to showcase your work.

Does the cohort guarantee a job or interview?

No. Resume sharing, MNC opportunities and investor interactions depend on selection, eligibility and performance, and none of them is guaranteed. We give you the environment and the opportunities to prove your ability.

How do I get fee and batch details?

Fill in the short form and a Trafy counsellor will share the details with you.
