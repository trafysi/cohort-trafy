Trafy opens applications for its 8-week AI Engineer Cohort

Trafy has opened applications for the AI Engineer Cohort, an 8-week, instructor-led programme that takes complete beginners from their first lines of Python to a deployed AI application.

Unlike a lecture series, the cohort is built around building. Across 16 live classes of two hours each (32+ hours of live teaching), participants work through Python, data handling, machine learning, generative AI, backend development with FastAPI, retrieval-augmented generation and AI agents. From Week 4 onwards they build: an AI chatbot, a full-stack AI application and a document-based knowledge assistant, before developing and presenting an individual capstone.

Every cohort member also gets free access to 30+ courses on the Trafy app and the Trafy Employability Index test, plus a Trafy profile to showcase their work. Resumes of top performers may be shared with FAANG companies, and the top three performers have the opportunity to join Trafy as paid interns. Resume sharing, industry opportunities and investor interactions depend on selection and performance, and none of them is a guarantee of an interview, a job or funding.

No prior programming experience is required. Applications are open now, and the full curriculum is available to download from the cohort page.
