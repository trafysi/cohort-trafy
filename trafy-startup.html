Startup track

Build something worth funding, with people who’ve actually done it

Trafy’s Startup Track isn’t a pitch competition. It’s a structured incubation path, from a raw idea to a working prototype to a real investor room, built around the people who can actually get you there.

Runs alongside the AI Engineer Cohort. Investor interactions are not a guarantee of funding.

The journey

A proper series of rounds, not a one-shot pitch

Three phases, nine checkpoints. Every idea that makes it through has already survived a real room.

Validate

01

Ideation

You bring the idea. We help you shape it.

  • Help turning a rough idea into a clear problem and solution
  • A first conversation with the Trafy team
02

Ideation Review

Pitch your idea to Trafy CTOs and investors. Get real feedback.

  • A live pitch to people who build and invest
  • Honest feedback, not a rubric
03

Idea Evaluation

We evaluate market potential, problem fit, team and feasibility.

  • A clear read on market potential and problem fit
  • Feedback on your team and feasibility

Build

04

C-Suite Mentorship

Get paired with a CEO, CTO, CMO or CFO mentor who guides your journey.

  • A mentor who has built and scaled before
  • Direct guidance on product, tech, growth and finance
05

Build & Validate

Build your MVP or prototype with expert guidance, tools and tech support.

  • Expert guidance while you build
  • Tools and technical support behind you
06

Prototype Milestone

Once you build a working prototype, you unlock the next level of Trafy incubation.

  • A working prototype in hand
  • Access to the funding phase: network, pre-seed and VCs

Fund

07

Networking Opportunities

Access our founder network, workshops, events and a strong peer community.

  • Trafy's founder network and peer community
  • Workshops and events
08

Pre-Seeding Support

Get help with your pitch deck, investor intros and your pre-seed round.

  • Pitch deck help
  • Investor introductions for your pre-seed round
09

VC Meetings

Meet and pitch our network of VCs and angels. We open doors, you close rounds.

  • Meetings with VCs and angels from our network
  • Support while you close your round
